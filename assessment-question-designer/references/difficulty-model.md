# Difficulty Model

Use a five-level model for technical and architectural assessment. Difficulty comes from reasoning
depth, not obscurity, verbosity, double negatives, or trick wording.

## Level 1 — Recognition / Basic Understanding

Tests a core definition, classification, or explicit principle in a fresh wording. Do not merely copy
the source sentence into a blank.

## Level 2 — Application

Presents a simple new scenario requiring application of one principle.

## Level 3 — Boundary Analysis

Requires distinguishing one boundary such as responsibility, scope, lifecycle, authority, or semantic
layer.

## Level 4 — Competing Plausible Interpretations

At least two options may be partially correct, but only one satisfies the stem's full scope, layer,
timing, authority, guarantee boundary, or other discriminating condition.

## Level 5 — Multi-principle Architecture Reasoning

Requires combining multiple independent principles, such as authority + lifecycle + capability
ownership, or safety + state freshness + cross-node dependency. The stem must supply the conditions
needed to make the answer determinate.

Level 5 is not a license for hidden assumptions. State the discriminating conditions and test whether
the learner can reason across them.
