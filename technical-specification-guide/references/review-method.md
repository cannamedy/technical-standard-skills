# Review Method

Review the actual artifact.

Do not review only:
- a revision report;
- a task-completion summary;
- a previous AI verdict;
- a list of claimed improvements.

## Direct review sequence

1. Read the candidate.
2. Read the source/golden master.
3. Compare real sections.
4. Identify losses.
5. Identify additions.
6. Identify terminology drift.
7. Inspect narrative continuity.
8. Inspect publication cleanliness.
9. Inspect numbering/cross-reference quality.
10. Decide whether the candidate is actually better.

Self-review is useful but is not independent acceptance.
