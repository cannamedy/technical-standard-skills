# v0.1 Failure Modes to Prevent

These are generalized failure modes observed while evolving a detailed PAIS guide.

## 1. Process language leaked into publication
Examples:
- calling the document "human-understanding";
- discussing the golden master;
- discussing the editing workflow;
- repeatedly mentioning the future contract.

## 2. "For beginners" became "simplify"
Useful technical content was removed instead of explained.

## 3. Fragmented prose
Local sections became clear while the whole document lost natural flow.

## 4. Taxonomy inflation
Simple distinctions expanded into too many labels.

## 5. Front-loaded concept fixes did not propagate
New Device/Node definitions were not reconciled with later Identity/Manifest language.

## 6. Teaching diagrams invented semantics
Illustrative flows risked being interpreted as protocol state machines.

## 7. Terminology drift
Examples used inconsistent names such as semantically different physical quantities.

## 8. Normative-strength drift
Natural-language rewriting risked changing SHOULD/MUST meaning.

## 9. Checklist acceptance
The document was declared ready because required items existed, despite numbering, flow, and prose issues.

These are Skill-level problems, not one-document-only problems.
