# Conceptual Expansion Budget

Do not solve reader confusion by creating excessive taxonomy.

## Rule

A new term is justified only when it:
- distinguishes behavior that matters;
- prevents a real architectural mistake;
- is already part of the architecture;
- will be reused meaningfully.

## Warning signs

- one original distinction becomes five terms;
- terms are described as "different dimensions" only after the new taxonomy is introduced;
- the reader needs a table just to understand the new explanatory vocabulary;
- the new term appears only once;
- the new term does not change implementation, reasoning, or boundary understanding.

## Preferred alternative

Explain the same idea in plain technical prose using existing terms.
