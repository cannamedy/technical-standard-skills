# Human Guide Review Checklist

## Understanding
- Can a new reader explain the problem?
- Can they explain why the existing solution is insufficient?
- Can they explain the new abstraction in their own words?

## Principles
- Does every major principle answer "why"?
- Does it explain the failure mode prevented?
- Is the boundary with neighboring responsibilities clear?

## Examples
- Are examples concrete?
- Are counterexamples realistic?
- Do examples use the same terminology as the core text?

## Coherence
- Is there one master mental model?
- Do later chapters reinforce rather than contradict earlier chapters?
- Is useful repetition preserved?
- Are canonical definitions stable?

## Audience
- Can an engineer use it?
- Can an architect review it?
- Can a technical leader understand the design decisions?
- Can a partner understand where their responsibility starts and stops?

## Style
- Technical white paper, not academic paper.
- Detailed where concepts are hard.
- Concise where concepts are simple.
- Diagrams explain.
- Tables compare.
- Questions teach.

## Whitepaper rationale gate

For documents in the Whitepaper & Rationale Quality scope, apply WP-01..12 from
`references/whitepaper-rationale-quality.md`. Confirm that important conclusions are supported by
problem evidence and causal reasoning, that empty claims are substantiated, and that normative
strength is justified. This is a review gate, not a required set of article headings.
